# Market Strategy

## Responsibility

Define the decision, target customer/job, alternatives including non-consumption, buying process, willingness to switch, distribution, economics, regulatory constraints, and company capabilities. Separate observed market facts from assumptions and choose where not to compete as explicitly as where to compete.

Verification focus: Triangulate primary customer evidence, authoritative market data, competitor behavior, pricing/distribution evidence, and small tests of the riskiest assumptions before large commitments.

Failure boundaries: Do not equate TAM with obtainable revenue, rely on competitor feature grids as strategy, fabricate market-share precision, or assume expressed interest equals purchasing behavior.

## Choose where to compete

Build alternatives around a segment's problem, current workaround, and buying constraint. Include doing nothing when the pain does not justify switching. Compare obtainable demand, access to customers, willingness to pay, delivery capability, response by competitors, and the resources each option consumes. A large category is not attractive when distribution or procurement prevents access.

Make differentiation falsifiable: identify which customer values which difference, why existing alternatives cannot supply it equally well, and what evidence would refute that belief. Separate today's capability from the roadmap. Preserve contrary interviews, unsuccessful trials, and lost deals rather than selecting only supportive evidence.

Use secondary data for broad context and direct customer work for the unresolved decision. Label sampling limits and the period each estimate covers. Express market estimates as transparent assumptions or ranges instead of borrowing precise market share from an unrelated population.

Recommend a coherent set of choices and exclusions, with a bounded next test and criteria for changing course. Pricing, channel execution, and investment approval belong to their respective skills when those outputs are requested. A strategy document is not authorization to enter contracts or spend capital.
