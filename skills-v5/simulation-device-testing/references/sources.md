# Source map

`simulation-device-testing` is individually authored. Promotion still requires held-out routing success, paired same-model skill/no-skill full-task runs, independent blind evaluation, and regression closure. The sources below bound version-sensitive statements in the skill; they do not convert a simulator result into physical proof.

## Version and conflict rules

- Resolve the exact firmware, simulator/emulator, board/device, driver, and fixture versions before applying setup details. A moving `latest` or `main` page is not the runtime identity.
- Do not infer that a board listed as supported by an emulator has accurate power, analog, RF, thermal, timing, or peripheral behavior beyond what the model actually implements.
- PX4 HITL support is version- and simulator-combination-sensitive. Perform a capability/preflight check for the pinned fixture rather than silently substituting a different simulator or branch.
- PX4's documented HITL injection path means SITL can exercise normal system paths that HITL bypasses. Evidence sufficiency is property-specific, not a one-dimensional fidelity ladder.
- When simulator and physical results diverge, preserve both results and investigate the model, fixture, calibration, timing, and implementation. Do not hide the divergence by changing tolerances after the fact.
- Facts about certification, environmental qualification, electrical limits, safety integrity, or regulatory compliance require the governing device/procedure sources; this package does not supply those claims.
