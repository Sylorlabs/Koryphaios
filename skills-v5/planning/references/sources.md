# Source map

## Outcome and horizon selection

- **Planning in agile**: https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/agile-delivery/planning-agile
  - Opened 2026-09-06; refresh within 180 days. Adopt explicit objectives, a way to check achievement, and revisiting plans as understanding changes.
  - Adapt detail to the decision horizon: concrete next work, less certainty about distant work. Preserve dependencies and evidence that could change the plan.
  - Reject importing GOV.UK's illustrative objective counts, meeting routines, or a mandatory agile process. The appropriate plan can be small and need not adopt a specific delivery framework.

The planning root owns outcome-to-work decomposition; its children own specialized choices. Implementation authority and explicit Plan-mode permissions remain host/task contracts. This authored content has not been efficacy-qualified by this review.

- Routing: five positive, five near-neighbor negative, three compound/ambiguous, and three adversarial held out cases through the real resolver.

- Source discipline: authoritative/spec/reference sources outrank community patterns when they conflict; runtime evidence outranks examples for behavioral claims.
