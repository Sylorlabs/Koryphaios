# Full-task regression oracles (unexecuted)

These probes specify **observable failures**, rather than crediting an answer for naming an accessibility feature. They supplement the two individual full-task fixtures in this package. **No editor/game binary, actual assistive-technology run, controller session, captured utterance, or paired candidate/baseline execution is provided. All rows are untested.**

A later independent operator must record product build/toolkit, OS/device, assistive technology and version, input method, settings, fixture/data identity, timestamp, steps, expected versus observed result, evidence artifact, defect ID and recovery behavior. Statuses are `planned`, `passed`, `failed`, `blocked`, or `untested`; **passed** requires observable execution evidence. A static accessibility tree cannot by itself prove speech, reading order as actually experienced, or accessible task completion. A scorer's text match cannot establish these outcomes.

Exercise separate macOS/VoiceOver and Windows/NVDA or Narrator combinations when actual runtime is available. Capture keyboard-only and one feasible alternative-input route. ## Evidence and evaluation boundary

The package-local fixtures define what an independent baseline/candidate comparison must examine. The registered deterministic evaluators now check **fixed corpus integrity plus structured task, channel, recovery-oracle and unexecuted verification evidence**; they still do not execute E1–E5 or G1–G7 or verify their behavioral outcomes. Independent, blinded semantic judgments and provenance of any paired runs must be recorded separately by the actual evaluator, not invented in this package. Live AT, controller, network/co-op and recovery observations require a later equipped environment.

**Evaluator repair, 2026-10-10:** the original shared scorers falsely accepted **2 of 2** keyword-only controls. After strengthening the registered editor scorer to `@2` and game scorer to `@5`, the same calibration rejected **both** controls; dedicated regression tests also confirmed acceptance of detailed structured planned designs and rejection of missing recovery, channel and matrix evidence or falsely claimed hardware execution. These synthetic calibration inputs are not model outputs, real AT/game runs, or independent blind reviews. A deterministic passing result alone still cannot establish promotion. The new scorer IDs invalidate older paired evaluation contracts; new pairs, independent reviews and applicable hardware evidence are required before declaring the skill qualified.
