# Contract Analysis

## Responsibility

Identify parties, term, scope, deliverables, payment, service levels, data/security/privacy, IP, confidentiality, warranties, indemnity, liability, termination, renewal, assignment, change control, disputes, governing law, and operational notice obligations. Distinguish plain-language extraction from legal conclusions.

Verification focus: Cross-reference defined terms, schedules/exhibits, incorporated documents, dates, caps/exceptions, survival clauses, and conflicting provisions; map material obligations to owners and flag items requiring qualified legal advice.

Failure boundaries: Do not invent missing clauses, treat summaries as legal advice, ignore exhibits/definitions, or state enforceability without jurisdiction-specific qualified review.

## Hierarchy and routing

- Broader skills: reference-communication.
- No narrower bundled child is declared.

## Preserve the agreement's structure

Establish the document set, parties, jurisdiction, transaction type, version, signature status, and order of precedence. Keep quotations and defined terms exact. A commercial agreement may include software, services, goods, or a mixture; a source about one transaction type does not establish rules for all of them.

For each material obligation, identify who must do what, for whom, by when, under which condition, with what consequence. Follow references into definitions, exhibits, policies, amendments, and incorporated terms. Distinguish an absent clause from a document that was not supplied.

Trace exceptions around liability caps, indemnities, warranties, termination, renewal, notice, data use, confidentiality, and surviving obligations. Calculate a deadline only from a known triggering date and the contract's counting rule. Preserve competing interpretations when wording or precedence is unresolved.

Return a source linked issue record with the clause, operational consequence, uncertainty, responsible reviewer, and a proposed question or negotiation objective. Separate plain language extraction from jurisdiction dependent enforceability or legal advice. Do not rewrite exact evidence to a house style, invent missing protections, send notices, accept terms, or sign on the basis of an analysis request.
