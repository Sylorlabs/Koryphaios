# Marketing Strategy

## Responsibility

Define target audience/job, category/frame, differentiated promise, proof, buying journey, channel fit, creative/message system, offer, budget/economics, measurement, and brand constraints. Match channel to audience behavior rather than chasing platform trends.

Verification focus: Use customer evidence, creative/message testing, channel-level incrementality where feasible, funnel quality, CAC/payback or mission-relevant outcomes, and brand/retention guardrails rather than impressions alone.

Failure boundaries: Do not manufacture customer claims, optimize vanity reach without downstream quality, use dark patterns, or attribute all conversions to the last measurable touchpoint.

## Hierarchy and routing

- Broader skills: market-competitive-research.
- No narrower bundled child is declared.

## Decisions and evidence

Choose the audience and intended behavior before allocating a channel budget. Map the buying journey from awareness to a qualified action and retained value. Explain why the message, proof, offer, and destination fit that audience. A high click rate with poor qualified conversion calls for a different decision from low reach with strong downstream outcomes.

Separate the creative claim from its substantiation. Review implied promises and material omissions as well as literal wording. Obtain product evidence before publishing capability or performance claims; a testimonial or money back offer does not establish general performance. Apply the relevant jurisdiction's advertising rules rather than treating one country's guidance as universal.

Define the learning objective, spend boundary, attribution limits, and stop or expand conditions for a campaign. Compare cohorts or controlled holdouts when causal claims are needed. Keep short term acquisition and longer term retention or brand effects separate. Audience consent and publication authority remain constraints even when a campaign is commercially attractive.

Deliver the audience rationale, message and evidence map, channel economics, measurement plan, and assumptions that would change the recommendation. Market research supplies evidence; this skill turns it into a marketing choice. It does not automatically authorize ad purchases, outreach, or publication.
