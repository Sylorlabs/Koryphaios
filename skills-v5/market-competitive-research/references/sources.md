# Source map

`market-competitive-research` is individually authored for V5; empirical qualification is tracked separately in the audit/evaluation ledger. These sources bound its market-evidence hierarchy, company/industry fact verification, current pricing/product evidence, and skill/eval design. Refresh all current product, pricing, financial, and market-statistic claims at task time.

## Qualification basis

- Package authorship is independent from empirical qualification; the audit/evaluation ledger is authoritative for qualification status.
- Hierarchy: broader research; narrower children market-strategy, marketing-strategy, pricing-monetization, procurement-vendor-evaluation.
- Routing: five positive, five near-neighbor negative, three compound/ambiguous, and three adversarial held out cases through the real resolver.
- Full-task evaluation: two individual scenarios with six falsifiable checks, same-model skill/no skill pairing, regression retention, and blind semantic judging.
- Source discipline: authoritative/spec/reference sources outrank community patterns when they conflict; runtime evidence outranks examples for behavioral claims.

## Interpretation rules

- Use first-party docs/pricing for current product facts, filings/audited reports for reported company facts, and official statistics for compatible market measures.
- Treat vendor positioning, customer logos, case studies, and benchmark claims as first-party assertions unless independently corroborated.
- Normalize price units, plan scope, included usage, overage, commitment, geography, and as-of date before comparing economics.
- Treat reviews, traffic, search interest, stars, social activity, and job postings as proxies, not direct proof of market share or customer satisfaction.
- Preserve unknowns and source conflicts explicitly; never turn mismatched category data into precise TAM or adoption claims.
