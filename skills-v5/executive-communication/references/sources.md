# Source map

`executive-communication` is individually authored for V5; empirical qualification is tracked separately in the audit/evaluation ledger. These sources bound audience-centered compression, key-takeaway-first writing, and skill/eval design. Decision facts, forecasts, risks, costs, and recommendations still come from task-specific verified evidence.

## Qualification basis

- Package authorship is independent from empirical qualification; the audit/evaluation ledger is authoritative for qualification status.
- Hierarchy: broader documents-communication.
- Routing: five positive, five near-neighbor negative, three compound/ambiguous, and three adversarial held-out cases through the real resolver.
- Full-task evaluation: two individual scenarios with six falsifiable checks, same-model skill/no-skill pairing, regression retention, and blind semantic judging.
- Source discipline: authoritative/spec/reference sources outrank community patterns when they conflict; runtime evidence outranks examples for behavioral claims.

## Interpretation rules

- Lead with the decision, status, outcome, or ask and write for scanning.
- Preserve the distinction between actuals, forecasts, scenarios, recommendations, and unknowns.
- Include only technical detail that changes impact, risk, cost, timing, or the decision.
- Tie every material number/status/date to the underlying evidence and as-of boundary.
- Make asks, owners, timing, and consequences explicit when the evidence supports them.
