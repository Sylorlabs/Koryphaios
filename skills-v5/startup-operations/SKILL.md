# Startup Operations

## Responsibility

Tie operating cadence to current stage and constraint: product learning, growth, delivery, hiring, cash, or reliability. Make ownership, decision rights, metrics, meeting purpose, documentation, hiring bar, vendor commitments, and runway implications explicit while preserving speed and reversibility.

Verification focus: Review whether each recurring process changes decisions or outcomes, track ownership/completion, reconcile cash/runway and capacity, and remove rituals whose information is unused.

Failure boundaries: Do not import big company process for status theater, optimize headcount as the goal, hide cash implications, or make founders the permanent routing layer for every decision.

## Design for the current constraint

Start with the decision or bottleneck, not an operating framework. Distinguish learning about demand, delivering commitments, developing capacity, and maintaining reliable operations. Assign an accountable owner and decision rights where work crosses functions, including the conditions that require escalation.

Close a compact operating contract before adding cadence. It should state the active constraint and decision, accountable owner and backup, decision rights, the observable trigger for act/hold/escalate, the dated cash or capacity envelope that bounds the choice, the smallest recurring record or meeting that consumes the signal, and the success, negative, recovery, and handoff conditions. Missing owners, thresholds, material inputs, or approval rules remain unresolved; do not turn them into defaults.

Choose a planning horizon consistent with cash, uncertainty, and commitments. Separate cash on hand, contracted obligations, expected receipts, and speculative financing. A single average burn rate can conceal a large renewal, delayed receipt, or hiring commitment; use dated scenarios when those events can change the decision.

Before recommending a hire, vendor commitment, recurring spend, or other operating change that is hard to reverse, test it against the agreed minimum cash/runway threshold using the full dated commitment, including start timing, fixed and variable cost, contracted obligations, and committed receipts. Pipeline and speculative financing do not satisfy a cash gate. If the base or required downside case breaches the threshold, or a material input is unknown, the decision is hold until the envelope changes or the evidence closes. For constraints that are not about cash, apply the same rule to scarce capacity: owner hours, coverage on call, displaced commitments, and delivery load must fit the explicit capacity envelope.

Give each recurring meeting or report a consumer and an intended decision. Replace status collection with a smaller durable record when no discussion is needed. Add process when repeated coordination failures justify it, and remove rules that no longer address the active constraint.

For reliability or customer operations work, make the operating sequence executable: initial response window, ongoing decision/communication cadence, recovery criteria, and handoff after the event, each with owners, evidence, approval boundaries, and an interruption path. Do not declare a communication sent, a recovery complete, or a handoff finished without the corresponding observable evidence.

## Finish the operating contract within the execution budget

When the caller supplies required artifacts, acceptance checks, or a time/tool/output budget, complete the smallest contract-complete artifact set first. Prefer a concise decision contract, dated trigger/envelope, owner/cadence table, and representative success, negative, and recovery evidence over exhaustive operating prose. Map material claims to supplied evidence or mark them unavailable, inferred, stale, or unresolved. Create or update required outputs early, with one path targeted once per edit operation. If a batched edit is rejected or targets the same path more than once, switch immediately to independent one-file writes; do not spend the remaining budget retrying the same output mechanism. Stop optional expansion once the remaining budget is needed to finish required artifacts and verification, then re-open those outputs before claiming completion.

Review actual outcomes against assumptions, including overdue ownership, delivery reliability, hiring capacity, and runway sensitivity. A recommendation to hire, spend, or reorganize does not authorize the transaction. Planning owns dependency and acceptance discipline; finance, legal, and people specialists own their respective regulated or professional judgments.
